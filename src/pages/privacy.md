_Last updated: September 12, 2026_

This policy explains how **Mayank Bhasin trading as Clarity AI** collects and uses personal information through this website and when providing mentoring, coaching, training, workshops, and digital resources.

## Information collected

Depending on how you interact with Clarity AI, I may receive:

- your name, email address, and contact details;
- information submitted through contact, application, or onboarding forms;
- appointment details and scheduling preferences;
- payment status, purchased product, billing contact details, and transaction references;
- CVs, portfolios, career goals, interview material, or project information you choose to share;
- correspondence, session notes, feedback, and support requests; and
- basic technical information needed to operate and secure the website.

Do not submit confidential employer, customer, health, financial-account, password, or security-sensitive information unless it is genuinely necessary and explicitly requested.

## Payment information

Payments are processed by Stripe. Clarity AI does not receive or store your full card number or card-security code. Stripe provides transaction information needed to confirm payment, issue receipts, administer subscriptions, prevent fraud, and handle refunds.

## How information is used

Personal information may be used to:

- respond to enquiries and assess whether a service is suitable;
- process purchases and administer subscriptions;
- schedule and deliver sessions, programmes, and resources;
- personalise mentoring and provide agreed feedback;
- maintain financial, tax, and business records;
- prevent misuse, fraud, or security incidents;
- improve Clarity AI services; and
- send marketing messages only where you have requested them or where otherwise permitted by law.

The applicable legal bases may include performing a contract, taking requested steps before a contract, complying with legal obligations, legitimate interests in operating and improving the service, and consent where required.

## Service providers

Information is shared only where reasonably necessary with service providers used to operate Clarity AI, which may include:

- Stripe for payments and subscriptions;
- Calendly for scheduling;
- email and video-conferencing providers;
- Vercel and GitHub for website hosting and deployment; and
- form, document, or productivity tools used to deliver an engagement.

These providers process information under their own privacy terms and may process data outside the United Kingdom using applicable safeguards.

Clarity AI does not sell your personal information.

## Retention

Information is kept only for as long as reasonably needed for the purpose for which it was collected. Financial and transaction records may be retained for the period required by tax and accounting law. Enquiry, onboarding, and session information may be deleted or anonymised when it is no longer needed to provide the service, manage a dispute, or meet a legal obligation.

## Testimonials and case studies

Your name, photograph, employer, testimonial, or identifiable results will not be published as a Clarity AI testimonial or case study without permission. Feedback may be used in anonymised and aggregated form where it does not identify you.

## Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights to:

- request access to personal information held about you;
- ask for inaccurate information to be corrected;
- request deletion or restriction;
- object to certain processing;
- withdraw consent where processing relies on consent; and
- request a portable copy of information you provided.

You may also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office if you believe your information has been handled unlawfully.

## Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to protect personal information. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure, so please share only information relevant to the requested service.

## Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as Clarity AI services and providers change. The current version and update date will remain available on this page.

Privacy questions and rights requests can be submitted through the [Clarity AI contact page](/contact).
